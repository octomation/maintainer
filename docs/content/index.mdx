## A small, practical release

The [v0.1.0 release](/changelog/v0.1.0/) starts with the contribution planning flow. The [calendar guide](/contributions/) explains dates, targets, and the other ways to inspect your activity; the Go and Makefile guides cover the smaller tools around your projects.

Maintainer works on the authenticated GitHub account for contribution commands. It reads the calendar; it does not create contributions or change your repositories.
